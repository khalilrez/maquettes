Weekly program · Rhythm & Taste

The week at Gondole

Seven nights, seven moods. The line-up changes every week — the new programme goes up on Instagram every Monday. Save the dates.

  1. LundiMonday

    The Freshest Kidz

    DJ Guirat sets the tone, with guests on deck. Fresh energy, same good vibes.

  2. MardiTuesday

    Laissez-moi chanter

    Karaoke night with Golden Team Karaoke — the mic is yours and the stage is waiting.

  3. MercrediWednesday

    Rake It Up

    Turning it up the right way, midweek.

  4. JeudiThursday

    Legacy

    Chambeh & guests — where sound meets story and every beat leaves a mark.

  5. VendrediFriday

    Gondole le Club

    Friday nights, reimagined: genre-bending guest sets that keep you moving all night long.

    also Soulmates

  6. SamediSaturday

    Guest nights

    A different invitation each Saturday — Warehouse Romance, an extended set with Hamdi Ryder, ITIZ WHAT IT IS…

  7. DimancheSunday

    Disco Bisco / Biscothèque

    Get ready to dance till late — or catch the Fantasy Club residents: no rules, just vibes, music & main character energy.

Every Monday, the week lands as one poster like this. Nights, guests and dates — reservation via Instagram.
