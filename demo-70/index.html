Weekly program · Beats & Bites

The week at La Marelle

Seven nights, seven moods. The line-up changes every week — the new programme is out every Monday. Save the dates.

  1. LundiMonday

    Fresh Faces

    Our resident DJ sets the tone, with guests on deck. Fresh energy, same good vibes.

  2. MardiTuesday

    Micro ouvert

    Karaoke night with our karaoke host — the mic is yours and the stage is waiting.

  3. MercrediWednesday

    Turn It Up

    Turning it up the right way, midweek.

  4. JeudiThursday

    Heritage

    Resident DJs & guests — where sound meets story and every beat leaves a mark.

  5. VendrediFriday

    Marelle Club

    Friday nights, reimagined: genre-bending guest sets that keep you moving all night long.

    also Kindred

  6. SamediSaturday

    Guest nights

    A different invitation each Saturday — warehouse sessions, extended sets, open-format nights…

  7. DimancheSunday

    Disco Sunday / Discothé

    Get ready to dance till late — or catch the Sunday residents: easy rules, good music & plenty of dancing.

Every Monday, the week lands as one poster like this. Nights, guests and dates — reservation via WhatsApp.
